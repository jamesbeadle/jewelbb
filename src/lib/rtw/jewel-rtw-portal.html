Start date is before 1 October 2026: a check is only legally required for employees. It is still good practice to run one - continuing records a voluntary check. A re-engagement after a break on or after 1 October needs a full check.

3Route ✓ Complete

Non-UK national - Home Office online check with share code (free)
British or Irish passport holder - manual passport check

The individual gets their share code at gov.uk/prove-right-to-work. You check it at gov.uk/view-right-to-work - when asked for the organisation carrying out the check, enter the engaging company's full name exactly. Codes are valid for 90 days.

You must be holding the original passport, with the individual present in person or on a live video call. The passport must be current - the draft Home Office guidance does not accept expired passports for manual checks. Copy every relevant page (photo, date of birth, nationality, signature, expiry) and mark the copy: "The date on which this right to work check was made: [date]".
